# odom→base at wheel rate

Task 715, 2026-11-04. `IcpOdomGate` now publishes `odom→base` at the wheel
odometry's 50 Hz instead of the lidar's 10 Hz. Nav2's controller returns to
20 Hz on Lyrical, in sim and on mote-01, with no Nav2 change. The cost is
5.3% of one Pi core, almost all of it in two Python nodes that hold a
`TransformListener`.

Raw outputs are in [`2026-11-04-odom-rate/`](2026-11-04-odom-rate/).

## The change

On Lyrical, Nav2 1.5.2's RotationShim waits on every control cycle for an
`odom→base` newer than `now()`. The gate published that edge once per scan, so
the controller ran at 10 Hz (`2026-11-03-lyrical-upgrade.md`).

The gate now does two things:

- **At each scan** it gates and accumulates kinematic_icp's increment exactly as
  before. The result is the *anchor*, the gated pose at the scan stamp.
- **At each wheel sample** (`/diff_drive_controller/odom`, 50 Hz) it publishes
  the anchor composed with the wheel motion since the scan stamp, stamped with
  the wheel sample's time.

This is a complementary filter: the wheels supply the motion the lidar has not
answered yet, and each scan discards the wheel motion it overlaps. There is no
EKF. kinematic_icp already fuses the wheels inside its registration, and its
covariance is a fixed parameter, so fusing downstream would count the wheels
twice with an invented weight.

Rules the code holds (`GatedOdometry` in `mote_nav/include/mote_nav/icp_odom_gate.hpp`, 16 new gtests):

- **Stamps only increase.** A scan's increment arrives after wheel poses later
  than its stamp have been published. So the scan only moves the anchor, and
  the next wheel sample publishes the corrected pose at its own, newer stamp.
  A TF reader asking for time *t* gets the estimate as it stood about 20 ms
  after *t*. The ICP correction for *t* appears at a later stamp.
- **The gate still decides.** A rejected increment is replaced by the wheel
  increment, held to the envelope, as before. The wheel propagation is clamped
  to the same envelope, so it is not a way past the gate.
- **Propagation stops 0.5 s after the last scan** (`max_propagation`). A lidar
  that stops answering leaves `odom→base` stale, as it did before, rather than
  running on wheels alone.
- **No wheel input means scan rate.** With no wheel samples the gate publishes
  at each scan, the previous behaviour.

The gate reads the wheel poses from the topic the relay inverts. It no longer
reads them through TF, so its `TransformListener` and its blocking lookup are
gone.

## The step at each scan

[`odom_steps.py`](../../mote_bringup/tools/odom_steps.py) measures the step from
outside the gate. For each consecutive pair of published poses, it takes the
residual between the later pose and the earlier one moved by the wheels over
the same interval. Between scans that residual is zero. At a re-anchor it is
the step a TF reader sees.

| source | steps | translation p50 / p99 / max | yaw p50 / p99 / max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| replay 192836 | 771 | 0.33 / 12.9 / 21.0 mm | 0.37 / 2.81 / 5.31° |
| replay 133149 (mostly parked) | 4,921 | 0.01 / 0.40 / 20.0 mm | 0.01 / 0.76 / 3.32° |
| replay 193037 | 1,562 | 0.23 / 1.70 / 12.1 mm | 0.28 / 2.50 / 4.78° |
| sim, office_world | 233 | 0.09 / 1.09 / 1.59 mm | 0.08 / 0.47 / 0.54° |
| mote-01, branch | 232 | 0.21 / 13.9 / 37.8 mm | 0.51 / 4.30 / 5.28° |
| mote-01, branch repeat | 237 | 0.21 / 13.3 / 18.4 mm | 0.41 / 3.42 / 3.74° |

**The step is not new error.** It is the per-scan disagreement between the
wheel increment and the gated ICP increment. The old 10 Hz edge carried the
same disagreement inside each increment. Scored the same way, the replay's
scan-rate track gives the identical distribution to the last digit
(`rate-results.txt`). On mote-01, main's 10 Hz edge scores p99 5.0–8.0 mm and
max 14 mm. The branch's 38 mm maximum is the scan after a 1.5 m/s excursion
the gate rejected: ICP's next increment gave part of the jump back while
staying inside the envelope. That is the gate's existing limit, not this
change's.

Consumers:

- **Costmaps**: the largest step measured, 38 mm, is under one 0.05 m cell. The
  p99 is a quarter of one.
- **AMCL**: it reads `odom→base` deltas between its updates. Those deltas carry
  every correction up to the earlier update, so they differ from the old ones
  by at most one scan's disagreement. Sim ATE improved, and every goal on
  mote-01 succeeded.
- **slip_monitor** reads the `base→odom_icp` leaf and the wheel leaf. Neither
  changed; `test_localization_composition.py` holds both.

## CPU on the Pi

`pixi run node-cpu`, 60 s at idle under `mote-robot.target`, two runs per
build ([`robot/cpu.txt`](2026-11-04-odom-rate/robot/cpu.txt)). `node_cpu.py`
cannot match the composable containers, because the environment installs
them, not this checkout. So [`proc_cpu.py`](2026-11-04-odom-rate/robot/proc_cpu.py)
samples those two from `/proc` over the same minute. Percent of one core:

| process | main | main (repeat) | branch | branch (repeat) | change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| task_server (Python) | 15.2 | 14.9 | 18.9 | 18.9 | +3.9 |
| slip_monitor (Python) | 13.9 | 14.0 | 16.0 | 15.9 | +2.0 |
| health_monitor (C++) | 2.5 | 2.5 | 2.6 | 2.6 | +0.1 |
| localization_container | 3.07 | 3.03 | 3.33 | 3.33 | +0.28 |
| nav2_container | 12.58 | 13.25 | 11.87 | 12.17 | −0.9 |

- The new edge adds 40 messages a second to `/tf`. Each Python node holding a
  `TransformListener` wakes for every one of them. task_server pays 3.9 points
  and slip_monitor 2.0. The C++ health monitor pays 0.1.
- The gate itself, in `localization_container`, costs 0.28 points more for
  five times the publications.
- nav2_container does not rise, though its controller runs twice as often.
- Net: 5.3 points of one core, out of the Pi's four.

task_server's listener exists for one precondition: `map→base_link` newer than
5 s. It could be answered from `/amcl_pose` without the `/tf` stream. That is a
follow-up, not part of this change.

## Offline tools

- `odom_steps.py` is new: it scores the step from any bag with `/tf` and the
  wheel odometry.
- `icp_gate_replay.py` feeds the gate wheel odometry instead of TF. It writes
  the scan-rate track for `odom_health.py`, and the 50 Hz broadcasts' steps to
  `<out>-steps.csv`.
- `bag_odometry.read_samples` now reads kinematic_icp from the
  `base_footprint→odom_icp` leaf when a bag has one. The `odom→base_footprint`
  edge has been the gated track since July, and is now wheel-propagated, so
  scoring it as the lidar would compare the wheels with themselves. Bags from
  before the gate carry no leaf and are read from the edge as before.

## Not measured

- One floor, one start pose on mote-01, 0.6 m legs. A long run with sustained
  turning at 2 rad/s, where the yaw benefit is largest, was not driven.
- Mapping mode. slam_toolbox reads `odom→base` as its prior; it was not
  re-benchmarked (`bag_replay` replays the recorded edge, so new bags will
  carry the wheel-rate one).
